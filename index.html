About

I am an Assistant Professor (tenure-track) in the Department of Agricultural Sciences at Clemson University and Water Economist at the South Carolina Water Resources Center. My research covers water resource economics, agricultural water management, and coastal community resilience.

I use experimental market design, discrete choice experiments, and empirical analysis to study how water is allocated, valued, and governed. Recent work covers irrigation adoption and land values, farmer and consumer acceptance of non-conventional water, and septic and wastewater policy on the South Carolina coast.

I received my Ph.D. (2023) and M.S. (2018) in Agricultural Economics from Purdue University, where my dissertation received the department's Outstanding PhD Dissertation Award. Before that, I earned a B.S. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from Yonsei University.
